Study mode

On a site in study mode, Study Engine asks instead of fixing: questions about your reasoning, nudges toward the precise word, and fact checks you fix yourself. Spelling and grammar still get direct fixes. Turn it on for a site from the assistant panel or the toolbar button.

Privacy

  • On your device: spelling, grammar and style rules, your dictionary, your glossary and the editor’s documents. These never leave your browser.
  • Sent to Anthropic, only with AI checks on: the paragraph being checked, or the text you ask about. Study Engine sends it straight to Anthropic’s API with your key, and to nowhere else. See Anthropic’s privacy policy.
  • Never: password fields, payment fields, or fields a site marks as off-limits for writing assistants. No analytics, no tracking, no ads.
